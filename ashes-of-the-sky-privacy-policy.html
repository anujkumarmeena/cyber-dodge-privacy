Privacy Policy for Ashes of the Sky

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Ashes of the Sky ("the App") is a mobile flight game developed by Anuj Kumar Meena.

Information We Collect

The current version of Ashes of the Sky does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Gameplay information, such as game progress or high scores, may be stored locally on your device to provide game functionality.

How We Use Information

Information stored locally by the App is used to provide and maintain gameplay features.

Data Sharing

The current version of the App does not intentionally sell personal information. Any data practices of third-party services or SDKs used in future versions will be described in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until it is cleared through the device's app settings, removed by the App, or the App is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

Ashes of the Sky is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. The current version does not maintain a server-side user account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at this public URL.
