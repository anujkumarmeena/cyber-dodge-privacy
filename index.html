Privacy Policy for Cyber Dodge

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Cyber Dodge ("the App") is a mobile arcade game developed by Anuj Kumar Meena.

Information We Collect

The current version of Cyber Dodge does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

The game may store gameplay information such as your best score locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the game's high-score functionality.

How We Use Information

Information stored locally by the game is used to operate and improve gameplay features such as saving your best score.

Data Sharing

The current version of Cyber Dodge does not intentionally transmit or sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data stored locally on your device remains there until it is removed by the App, cleared through your device's app settings, or the App is uninstalled.

Because the current version does not require an online account, there is no server-side account or personal data that users need to request deletion of.

Children's Privacy

Cyber Dodge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because the current version does not transmit personal information to our servers, there is no personal information stored by us on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be made available at the same public URL.
